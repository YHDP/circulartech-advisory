Digital Product Passports in short

What is a Digital Product Passport?

A Digital Product Passport is a structured data record about a product, reached through a data carrier such as a QR code. EU product rules make it mandatory product group by product group. It carries the information that authorities, repairers, recyclers and buyers need, such as composition, origin and how to repair or recycle the product.

Which products need a passport, and from when?

Batteries come first: electric-vehicle batteries, light-transport batteries and industrial batteries above 2 kWh need a passport from 18 February 2027 under the Battery Regulation. Other groups follow once the Commission adopts their delegated acts under the Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation. Its first working plan names textiles, furniture, mattresses, tyres, iron and steel, and aluminium. Until a product group has its delegated act, there is no passport date for it.

Where does a company start?

With a readiness assessment. It shows which passport rules apply to your products and when, which of the required data you already hold, and which systems have to host, protect and register the passport. The compliance roadmap then puts that work in order against your deadline.
